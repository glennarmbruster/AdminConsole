Written 2026-08-13. Replaced the same day, before either was run against real
data. Parked rather than deleted -- Glenn asked for them to be removed from the
DLMigration folder; this is the undo copy.

WHY THEY WERE REPLACED

They scoped to one directory and enriched every row -- member counts, nested
group detection, resolved owners. That is one AD bulk read plus a chunked LDAP
pass plus an owner lookup per distinct ManagedBy, and all of it before you can
look at a single row.

The scoping turned out to be the weak part. Exchange has to search the whole
forest to answer Get-DistributionGroup anyway, so filtering to one directory
afterwards saved nothing at query time and cost the ability to see the rest of
the estate. If the expensive part happens regardless, pull everything once and
slice it afterwards.

Replaced by Inventory-AllGroups.ps1: every mail-enabled group, three fields,
no AD calls at all.

WORTH KEEPING IF THE ENRICHMENT IS WANTED LATER

  Get-GroupDnLookup      chunked LDAP nested-group detection, routed per
                         directory. Fifty DNs per filter instead of one query
                         per member.
  Get-OwnerText          ManagedBy resolved to name + address, cached.
  Get-HqGroupInventory   the Exchange/AD join on distinguishedName.

The directory-scoping helpers those relied on are NOT here -- they live in
Common.ps1 (ConvertTo-DomainDn, Get-DirectoryDomainDn,
Resolve-DistinguishedNameDirectory, Test-DnInDirectory) and are documented in
ARCHITECTURE section 32, including the child-domain longest-suffix trap.
